\section{Additional Study: ReForm-as-Seeder (CombiBench100, @16)}

\paragraph{Goal.}
This study treats a strong single-trajectory system (ReForm) \emph{only} as a seed provider: we take its generated statements as the seed bank, and then run FormalEvolve as a plug-in handover mechanism under a strict call budget.
The key question is not token-parity; instead we ask whether population-based compile-gated search reduces cross-problem concentration (lower Gini / lower top-10\% share) under a tight call budget.
